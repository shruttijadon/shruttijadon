## Research Abstract

My research focuses on developing **transparent and domain-specific Explainable AI (XAI)** systems that can support real-world decision-making in complex industrial environments.  

I am particularly interested in applying machine learning and time-series forecasting techniques to **maritime logistics, smart port operations, digital twin systems, and sustainability analytics**. My goal is to design interpretable AI frameworks that not only deliver accurate predictions but also provide clear explanations to domain experts, enabling trustworthy and practical adoption in industrial settings.

Through projects such as **MARITIME-X**, I have explored how Explainable AI can be used to predict vessel delays, estimate carbon emissions, and support operational decision-making in port environments. I aim to further advance this direction by integrating digital twin concepts, multi-modal data, and robust XAI techniques for next-generation smart logistics and industrial AI systems.
